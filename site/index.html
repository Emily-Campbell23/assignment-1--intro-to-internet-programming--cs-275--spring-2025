Dieter Rams's Ten Principles for Good Design

  • Innovative
  • Useful
  • Aesthetic
  • Understandable
  • Unobtrusive
  • Honest
  • Long-lasting
  • Detailed
  • Environmentally Friendly
  • Minimal

1. Good Design Is Innovative

Technological development always offers new opportunities for original designs. Imaginative design develops in tandem with improving technology.

2. Good Design Makes a Product Useful

A product must satisfy functional, psychological, and aesthetic criteria. Good design emphasizes usefulness while disregarding anything that could detract from it.

3. Good Design Is Aesthetic

The aesthetic quality of a product is integral to its usefulness. Well-executed objects can be beautiful and improve well-being.

4. Good Design Makes a Product Understandable

It clarifies the product’s structure and function, making it intuitive and, ideally, self-explanatory.

5. Good Design Is Unobtrusive

Products should act as tools rather than decorative objects, maintaining a neutral and restrained design.

6. Good Design Is Honest

It does not make a product appear more innovative or valuable than it truly is, avoiding deception.

7. Good Design Is Long-Lasting

It avoids trends and remains relevant over time, unlike fashionable or disposable designs.

8. Good Design Is Thorough Down to the Last Detail

Nothing is left to chance; care and precision show respect for the user.

9. Good Design Is Environmentally Friendly

It conserves resources and minimizes waste and visual pollution.

10. Good Design Is as Little Design as Possible

Less, but better. It focuses only on the essential aspects, avoiding unnecessary elements.
